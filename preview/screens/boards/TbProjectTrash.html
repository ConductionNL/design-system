How deleting works

Deleting hides it here and keeps it for 30 days under Deleted items; OpenProject still has it until then. Delete for good, or the end of the 30 days, deletes it in OpenProject. Restore puts a column or a work package back where it was, with its comments, mails and meetings. Nothing changes in OpenProject. A work package with child work packages restores, or is deleted for good, together with them. A column is never deleted: it is removed from this board, its status stays in OpenProject. OpenProject has no deleted items of its own, so this list is kept in Nextcloud.
