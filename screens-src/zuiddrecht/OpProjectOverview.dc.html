Rollout of OpenProject and Nextcloud for Ville de Lyon, 1,200 users in two phases. Phase 1 (pilot, 80 users) went live on 14 September. Phase 2 covers all departments and goes live on 2 November, with single sign-on through the Lyon Keycloak realm and project folders linked from Nextcloud.

Customer contact is Marc Dubois. Weekly review on Thursday at 14:00; the phase 2 kick-off moves to the afternoon on his request.
