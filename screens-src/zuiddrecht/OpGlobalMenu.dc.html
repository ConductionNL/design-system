The global menu

Apps and modules behind the all apps button, as Nextcloud draws it

OpenProject's top bar is the Zuiddrecht workplace bar. The brand block names the environment and the product or module you are in; the all apps button after it opens the complete list, Nextcloud above OpenProject, each in three columns. There is no row of icons in the bar.

1 · At 1440, closed

Brand block 237 px with the emblem and two lines, a 1 px divider, the all apps button 40 x 40, search 560 px, then quick add, notifications, contacts and the account button. The second line of the brand block says where you are.

2 · At 1440, the all apps button pressed

The complete map: every app and module, the Nextcloud group above the OpenProject group, each flowing in three columns, the current one filled. "Manage apps" shows only for an admin.

3 · Below 1240 px, narrow

The account button keeps the avatar and chevron and drops the name and role; the search shrinks to its 240 px minimum. Everything else stays where it is. Drawn at 1100 px.

Three levels, each in its own place

  1. Global menu behind the all apps button: apps and modules, the same on every board; the brand block names where you are.
  2. Project menu in the sidebar: 264 px, the modules of the project you are in.
  3. Module pages nested under the module: saved views, boards and wiki pages expand in place under their item.
