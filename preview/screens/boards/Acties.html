Rules that keep it simple

  1. One primary button per page. If two actions compete, the step decides.
  2. At most three quick actions, the same three on every case: message, document, log contact.
  3. "Meer" is grouped and worded by what it does to the case. No more than 12 entries.
  4. Admin and debug actions are shown to admins only, in their own group at the bottom.
  5. Help links live in the app's help menu, not on every record. "Refresh" goes: the page refreshes itself.
  6. A tab shows its count. An empty tab moves under "Meer".
  7. The side column holds facts, never actions, except one small action per fact card.
