Access and limits

A limit applies to everyone and cannot be changed by a user.

Who may use the calendar

Approve the calendar for the whole organisation, or start with a few groups. Nobody outside the approved groups sees the app, and no outside account can be connected until the calendar is approved.

Force new reminders to be

With a forced kind, every new reminder a user adds is that kind. Existing reminders stay as they are.
