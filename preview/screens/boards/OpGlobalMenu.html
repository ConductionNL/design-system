The global menu

Apps and modules as a row of icons in the top bar

Nextcloud's positioning: the first seven apps and modules sit in the bar after the logo, the nine-dot button at the end of the row opens the complete list. The current item carries its label and a white underline; the others show their label on hover and focus.

Three levels, each in its own place

  1. Global menu in the bar: apps and modules, the same on every board.
  2. Project menu in the sidebar: 264 px, the modules of the project you are in.
  3. Module pages nested under the module: saved views, boards and wiki pages expand in place under their item.
