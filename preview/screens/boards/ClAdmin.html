Access and limits

A limit applies to everyone and cannot be changed by a user.

Who may use the calendar

Switch the calendar on for everyone, or for a few groups first. Nobody outside those groups sees the app, and no outside account can be connected until the calendar is on for everyone.

Kind of reminder users may add

With one kind only, every new reminder a user adds is that kind. Existing reminders stay as they are.
