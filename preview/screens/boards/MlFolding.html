The mailbox folds around what you do

The width goes to the one thing you are doing, browsing, reading or acting, and never more than three things sit side by side.

Four rules

  1. 1Never more than three things side by side: the rails, the content, and at most one full column next to it.
  2. 2A rail is the same column, folded. The panel icon at its top opens it, and the open column folds with the same icon. Hovering a rail shows the full column over the content without moving the layout.
  3. 3Any right panel folds the conversation list. The X at the top right of a panel closes it and returns you to reading; the X at the end of the subject bar closes the conversation and returns you to browsing.
  4. 4The work package is a chip in the subject bar, with its status. One click opens its panel, which counts as a right panel.
