At most ten

A menu shows the work of one role. Ten entries is the ceiling, in three groups.

One start

Every app opens on the same three: Dashboard, Mijn werk, Wachtrij. An employee learns it once.

Views, not pages

A filtered list is a saved view with a count, not a menu entry of its own.

Set-up is not work

Types, templates, rules and logs live in Geavanceerd. Help links live in Hulp.
