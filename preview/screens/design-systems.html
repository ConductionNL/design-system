Example design systems.

Five fictional organisations, one canon. The demo municipality Zuiddrecht and four schools in the same town each get a full house style: colours with checked contrast, a typeface, a logo set and a motif. On the screens gallery any of them can restyle every Zuiddrecht screen.

Screens are referenced by a stable id, the app and the board name: portaliq/MijnZaken, wilgenboom/Home. Use that id in conversation and in issues.

Primary school, ages 4 to 12

Basisschool De Wilgenboom

The tree you shelter under. Warm, calm and clear for busy parents, friendly without being childish. Parents report a child absent, read news from the group, book a parent evening and see report cards in Mijn Wilgenboom. Lexend was designed for reading ease, which is the school's subject.

Vocational college

Esdoornveen, mbo college

Learning that leads to a trade. Practical and grown-up: students are 16 and older and half their learning happens in a company. Students log work-placement hours and see assessments; the company approves hours. It feels closer to a professional tool than to a school. IBM Plex Sans for text, Plex Mono for codes.

Training for heat-pump installers

Warmtepompacademie

Heat moves from cold to warm. A trade academy for installers: exact, technical, no fuss. Participants and their employers want a date, a place, a price and a certificate. They find a course, enrol employees and download certificates. Barlow Semi Condensed sets headings and numbers, Barlow the text.
