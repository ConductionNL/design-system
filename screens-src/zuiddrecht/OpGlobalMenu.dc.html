The global menu

Apps and modules as a row of icons in the top bar

Nextcloud's positioning: the first seven apps and modules sit in the bar after the logo, the nine-dot button at the end of the row opens the complete list. The current item carries its label and a white underline; the others show their label on hover and focus.

1 · At 1440, closed

Brand block 264 px wide, divider on the sidebar edge, then Files, Mail, Calendar, Work packages, Boards, Gantt, Meetings and the nine-dot button. Home is current here, so it takes the last place in the row. Search keeps at least 320 px.

2 · At 1440, the nine-dot button pressed

The complete map: every app and module, the seven in the row too, grouped Nextcloud and OpenProject, the current one filled. "Manage apps" shows only for an admin.

3 · Below 1240 px, collapsed

The row folds into the nine-dot button, which then sits right after the divider, where Nextcloud keeps its waffle. The current item stays beside it so the bar still says where you are. Drawn at 1100 px.

Three levels, each in its own place

  1. Global menu in the bar: apps and modules, the same on every board.
  2. Project menu in the sidebar: 264 px, the modules of the project you are in.
  3. Module pages nested under the module: saved views, boards and wiki pages expand in place under their item.
