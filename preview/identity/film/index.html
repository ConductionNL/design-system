Film

Every frame on purpose.

Our films are web pages. Each frame is computed from the time, on a 128 BPM grid at 24 frames a second. This section holds the method, the motion tokens, kinetic type on a voice, and the brief and review we use for every film.

The five laws

  1. One ideaIt fits one sentence. If the logline needs "and", it is two films.
  2. One device that travelsThe thing the eye follows crosses every cut. It never blinks out.
  3. One orangePer scene, orange is the answer. Text or a shape, never a box behind text.
  4. One motion systemMoves come from named tokens. Times come from the film's timeline.
  5. Measure, never guessTimes from the grid or the voice, holds from the word count, loudness from the meter.

The house rules, short

Picture

  • Solid fills. No gradients, glows, blur, 3D or photos.
  • Pointy-top hexes, never rotated. A hex flips in, it never pops.
  • One honeycomb grid, solid. Every hex sits on a cell.
  • Type in a left column, the picture on the right.

Words and sound

  • The story reads with the sound off. Frame 1 says something.
  • 20 to 30 words on screen, at most 35 per 30 seconds.
  • Each line holds 1.5 seconds, or 0.4 seconds a word if that is longer.
  • Every sound has a visible cause. The accent sound is a dry click.

The binding rules are in the film bible, preview/films/BIBLE.md, rounds 1 to 29c. The method is the film skill in the design-system repository, at .claude/skills/film/.
