Phase 2 rollout plan

Version 7 · last edited by Olivia Brandt, today 09:40 · History · Watch

Phase 2 brings all departments of Ville de Lyon onto OpenProject and Nextcloud on Monday 2 November 2026. Sign-in goes through the Lyon Keycloak realm, and every project gets a linked folder in Nextcloud so files stay in one place. This page is the plan of record; the agenda for the weekly review lives in Meetings.

Scope

  • Single sign-on for 1,200 users, tracked in #58390.
  • Project folders linked from Nextcloud, tracked in #58412.
  • Mail archive stays on the Lyon server; the link stores a reference only.
