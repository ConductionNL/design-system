Static assets the swizzle expects

  • /lib/canal-footer.css scoped under .canal-footer
  • /lib/canal-footer.js randomises the skyline + drives the boat-game
  • Templates for houses (5) + ships (4) ride below the footer as raw HTML so canal-footer.js can .content-clone them — the swizzle injects them via dangerouslySetInnerHTML.

Footer · the other 95 percent of sites

A footer you can build in one file

The canal scene above is for conduction.nl. It needs a canvas, two scripts and a set of SVG templates, and it exists because the company site can afford a signature moment. Every other surface, an app, a docs site, a client portal, a print-adjacent page, needs a footer that is just a footer.

This is that footer. No JavaScript, no images, no canvas. Every colour reads from a published token, so it inherits the brand without hardcoding a single hex, and it follows a theme change on its own.

Use the canal footer

conduction.nl only. It is the brand's set piece and loses its meaning if it turns up on every site.

Use the plain footer

Everywhere else. Apps, docs, portals, sub-brands, anything a partner builds on our stack.

The tokens it reads

Four tokens carry the whole thing. Point them somewhere else and the footer follows, including into dark mode, where they are already mapped.

--conduction-color-footer-background   the ground
--conduction-color-footer-title        column headings
--conduction-color-footer-link         link labels and the wordmark
--conduction-color-footer-text         body copy and the legal bar

Markup

One <footer>, a grid of columns, a legal bar. Add or drop columns freely; the grid reflows.

Rules that travel with it

Column headings are nouns, not sentences. Four columns at most, or the grid stops being scannable. The legal bar carries the entity name, KvK number and the year, because that is a legal requirement rather than a design choice. Orange is the hover colour and nothing else: one accent per component still applies, and here the accent is spent on hover.
