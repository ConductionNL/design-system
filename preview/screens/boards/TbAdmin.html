Tasks

What holds for the Tasks app of everyone on this workspace: who may create a project, whether projects can be shared with other servers, and how long deleted items are kept.

Projects, work packages and members come from OpenProject. These settings are kept in this app. The first one also limits who creates projects in OpenProject from here.

Who may create projects

Deleted items

Deleting a work package hides it on the board and keeps it for 30 days under Deleted items of its project, where a member can restore it. OpenProject still has it until then. A column is never deleted, only removed from the board.

days

Delete for good, or the end of these days, deletes it in OpenProject. 0 keeps deleted items until a member deletes them for good.
