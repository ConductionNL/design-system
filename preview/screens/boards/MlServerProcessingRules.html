Processing rules

Every message that arrives or leaves passes the rules below in this order, top to bottom. Each rule reads in plain words: when this, then that.

Rules of Northwind Projects. A rule that is switched off stays in the chain but does nothing.

2 rules rewrite mail for sales@northwind-consulting.test, in this order: 3  Rewrite consulting addresses 5  Route support mail by directory Result: support@support.northwind-projects.test

Rule 4: Stop customer data leaks

Applies to mail of northwind-projects.test

When

Combine conditions with and, or and not
  1. When
    Message leaves from northwind-projects.test
  2. and
    Matches data leak pattern Citizen service number, pattern of northwind-projects.test
  3. or
    Matches data leak pattern Bank account number, pattern of northwind-projects.test
  4. and not
    Company directory says sender is a member of Finance

Then

  1. Hold the message and tell the sender why it was stopped
  2. Add the header X-Northwind-Held: data-leak
