Design note, not a screen: not for the user test deck

What happens to a link

A link joins a conversation, or one message, to a work package by storing a reference: the mail stays in your mailbox, and the work package only ever points at it. This is what that means over time.

  1. 1

    You link

    From the conversation, press Link to work package or the L key, pick the work package and choose the scope: the whole conversation or this one message. Nothing is copied, the mail stays where it is.

    In OpenProject: The work package gets an EMAILS tab next to FILES. It lists the conversation with sender, subject, snippet, date, message count, attachments and who linked it.

  2. 2

    A reply arrives

    A reply in a linked conversation is linked too, without you doing anything. If you linked one message only, a reply is not linked and shows the suggestion instead.

    In OpenProject: The entry on the EMAILS tab shows the new message count and the latest snippet, and the activity gets a line if you chose to log replies.

  3. 3

    You forward

    Forwarding starts a new conversation to new people. It does not inherit the link. The work package number in the subject is detected, so the new conversation shows "Looks like Feature #58412. Link?"

    In OpenProject: Nothing changes until you confirm the suggestion. Then the forwarded conversation is listed as a second entry.

  4. 4

    You move or archive

    Moving the conversation to another folder, or archiving it, keeps the link. The reference follows the message, not the folder.

    In OpenProject: The entry stays as it was. Reply still opens the conversation in your mail client, wherever it now lives.

  5. 5

    You delete the mail

    Deleting the mail from your mailbox removes the content for everyone. The link is not silently dropped: it turns into a trace that says what happened and when.

    In OpenProject: One line remains: "Mail deleted by its owner on 12 Oct". Reply and the attachments are gone with it.

  6. 6

    Someone without access looks

    A colleague who cannot read your mailbox sees that the conversation exists, not what it says. The body, the attachments and Reply stay hidden from them.

    In OpenProject: Subject, sender and date are shown, the body is not, with the line "Ask Olivia Brandt for access".
