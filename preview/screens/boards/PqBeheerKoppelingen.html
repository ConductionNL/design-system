Incoming messages

SMS and WhatsApp providers call Pipelinq without a Nextcloud login. After the signature checks out, Pipelinq saves what they send as the account below.

Incoming messages, conversations, STOP replies and messages from unknown numbers are saved as this account. It joins the group pipelinq-messaging, which may create and change messages, conversations and contact moments.

Incoming messages are now saved as pipelinq-berichten.
