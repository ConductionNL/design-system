The Advanced foldout

The menu shows the work. Everything an agent needs now and then, and everything that sets the app up, waits one click away under Advanced, the last entry in the sidebar footer. It opens in place, under the entry, and pushes nothing else away. "Modules and more" used to be a menu entry of its own; on Ruben's decision it moved out of the menu into this foldout, where it is now called Modules.
