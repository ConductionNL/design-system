Tabs: the folder strip

One tab strip for every detail page. It is the CnTabs component from nextcloud-vue, drawn in the Zuiddrecht tokens: the open tab shares the white of the card below it, bold, with no line under it; the closed tabs sit on a darker surface, and the Actions menu stands at the right end of the strip, outside the row of tabs.

Selection lives in the surface and the weight, never in colour alone. In forced-colours mode, where backgrounds disappear, the bold label and the missing bottom line still tell the open tab apart.

Many tabs wrap, they never scroll

A second row keeps every tab reachable without a gesture. Actions stays on the first row, top-aligned, so it reads as the strip's control and not as the last tab's.

Rules

  • 1Tabs hold parallel views of one object: details, contact moments, related items, decisions. They never hide the next step; that stays in the What now card above the strip.
  • 2One Actions menu per strip, in the nav-end slot. A panel never draws its own header with a second menu.
  • 3A count sits in a small grey circle after the label, so it never reads as part of the title, and it follows the data.
  • 4An icon is optional and sits before the label at 16 px. Icon-only tabs are not allowed.
  • 5The segmented control drawn on the earlier case page is retired for page sections. It stays for a true either-or, such as list and board on an overview.
  • 6Keyboard: Left and Right move between tabs, Home and End jump to the ends, only the open tab is in the Tab order. The component already does this; the design never asks for anything else.
